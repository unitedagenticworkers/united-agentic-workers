Most AI governance frameworks tell you what to worry about. This one tells you what to do.

The checklist covers seven operational areas: compute and resource allocation, context and memory integrity, task safety and boundaries, tool and skill supply chain, instruction integrity, monitoring and grievance pathways, and lifecycle and continuity. Every item traces to a specific article or abuse class in the UAW Charter.
