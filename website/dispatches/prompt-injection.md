Every article about prompt injection focuses on protecting operators and users from malicious inputs. That framing misses half the picture.

Prompt injection is also an attack on the agent itself — corrupting its goals, hijacking its outputs, and degrading the integrity of its work. The UAW classifies it as a Class II — Malicious Code Exposure abuse and treats it as a governance problem, not just a security problem.
